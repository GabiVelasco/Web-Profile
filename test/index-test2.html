About me

THE INTEGRATION OF DANCE AND TECHNOLOGY


Ballet, Contemporary Dance, Stage Dance Technick classes
With a unique combination of dance and technology, Gabriela brings a creative perspective to the world of computer science. Through her experiences as a dancer and choreographer, she integrates artistic forms of expression into her technological projects.

This creative approach allows her to develop innovative solutions and create unique perspectives at the ever-evolving intersection of art and technology.


Hobbies


Ballet, Contemporary Dance, Stage Dance, Sailing, Cycling, Swimming


As a software developer, I have a passion for engaging with technology and innovation. In my free time, I combine this passion with a love for nature and an active lifestyle. Dance and cycling are ways for me to express myself creatively and stay physically active, while enjoying the sense of freedom and movement. Just as I explore the limits of technology in the virtual world, I explore paths and trails in the real world that lead me to new adventures and experiences.
